# R1: Hunting App Teardown

_Researcher, 2026-10-23. Milestone 1, task R1. Sources were checked on this date. Prices change often, so recheck before quoting them to the crew._

**Legend:** `[unverified]` = no source confirmed it. `[vendor]` = the source is the company itself or a paid or sponsored piece. `[old]` = the source is more than two years old.

## Summary
- All five apps are **solo-scouting map tools first**. Group features are limited to sharing pins or areas. None of them builds a **season story** (roster, harvest board, stories, awards) the way Deer Camp would.
- **Private pins** are the default everywhere because pins stay on the user's account until they share them. Sharing is usually a **one-way copy by link**, not a living private group map. onX "Collaborative Folders" and HuntStand "Group Hunt Areas" come closest.
- **Wind and weather is a crowded space:** HuntStand HuntZone, HuntWise WindCast and HuntCast, and Spartan Forge AI forecasts. Deer Camp should not try to compete on prediction.
- **Offline maps are standard and paid in every app.** Users complain most about offline downloads failing and data lost after updates.
- **Harvest logging is weak or missing** in the big five. BaseMap has a journal with weather-stamped markers. The others treat waypoints as the log.

## onX Hunt
- **What and who:** The market-leading hunting GPS and land-ownership map, aimed at public- and private-land hunters ([onX pricing](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/pricing)).
- **Pricing:** Basic is free. Premium is $34.99/yr for one state and $49.99/yr for two. Elite is $99.99/yr or $14.99/mo ([onX pricing](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/pricing)).
- **Key features:** About 100 waypoint icons, with photos and notes on waypoints. onX pitches waypoints as "your hunt log" ([onX waypoints](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/features/waypoints)).
- **Pin privacy:** Markups stay on the user's account until shared. The page describes no separate private-only setting. Collaborative folders let the owner choose who can contribute and who can only view ([onX waypoints](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/features/waypoints)).
- **Group use:**
  - Users share waypoints, tracks, areas, routes and folders by link (text or email). Collaborative Folders "build, share, and sync your hunt plan with your crew."
  - Sharing requires a paid membership ([onX sharing](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/learn/capabilities/sharing)).
- **Harvest logging:** There is no dedicated harvest log, only waypoints with photos and notes ([onX waypoints](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/features/waypoints)). onX does not say whether weather is recorded with a waypoint `[unverified]`.
- **Weather and wind:** Included in Premium ([onX pricing](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/pricing)). Feature depth not checked `[unverified]`.
- **Offline:**
  - Offline maps come with Premium ([onX pricing](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/pricing)).
  - **Share Nearby** sends markups to nearby phones with no signal, but only iPhone to iPhone or Android to Android.
  - Edits made after an offline share do **not** sync to the other phone ([onX offline sharing](https://www.onxmaps.com/hunt/app/features/offline-sharing)).
- **Praise:** Users praise it as the standard for land and boundary info and in-field navigation ([Kimola review summary](https://kimola.com/reports/optimize-your-outdoor-adventures-with-onx-hunt-user-insights-app-store-us-144952); [Outdoor Life 2019](https://www.outdoorlife.com/tested-best-hunting-apps-for-hunters/) `[old]`).
- **Complaints:** Cost, upgrade pop-ups, boundaries off by about an acre and slow to fix, a billing charge after a trial, and outdated imagery ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/optimize-your-outdoor-adventures-with-onx-hunt-user-insights-app-store-us-144952)). Forum threads say the free Basic tier has been cut back ([Rokslide thread](https://rokslide.com/forums/threads/onx-basic-now-totally-useless.379186/latest)). The thread could not be loaded, so the details are `[unverified]`.

## HuntStand
- **What and who:** A free hunting and land-management map from Good Sportsman Marketing, with 9M+ downloads. It is aimed at whitetail hunters and landowners ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id778772892)).
- **Pricing:**
  - The App Store lists Pro at $24.99–$34.99, Pro Whitetail at $69.99, Ultimate at $99.99/yr, and ad-free at $5.99 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id778772892)).
  - A competitor's comparison gives Pro at $34.99 and Ultimate at $99.99, with a 7-day trial ([HuntWise vs HuntStand, Oct 2026](https://huntwise.com/field-guide/hunting-tips/huntwise-vs-huntstand-hunting-app-comparison) `[vendor: competitor]`).
- **Key features:** Property lines, rut maps, game activity forecasts and a digital rangefinder ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id778772892)).
- **Pin privacy:** Pins are private until shared. Group Hunt Areas share a map with "buddies, lease partners, and family" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id778772892)). Roles and permissions are not documented in sources found `[unverified]`.
- **Group use:**
  - A family managing 180 acres uses shared stands, cameras and plots so each member can find the others' marks ([Union Sportsmen's Alliance](https://unionsportsmen.org/huntstand-land-management-gets-a-boost-from-a-21st-century-app/) `[vendor partner]`).
  - One club tried HuntStand for stand sign-in, then **went back to paper** because members did not keep it up ([GON forum, 2025](https://forum.gon.com/threads/vsib-anyone-else-using-it.921401/page-2)).
- **Harvest logging:** Users can track "sightings and harvests" ([USA](https://unionsportsmen.org/huntstand-land-management-gets-a-boost-from-a-21st-century-app/)). Depth not documented `[unverified]`.
- **Weather and wind:** HuntZone (free) shows hour-by-hour scent and wind impact up to 72 hours ahead. A 15-day Whitetail Activity Forecast needs Ultimate ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id778772892); [HuntWise comparison](https://huntwise.com/field-guide/hunting-tips/huntwise-vs-huntstand-hunting-app-comparison)).
- **Offline:** Unlimited offline maps come with Pro ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id778772892)).
- **Praise:** Rated 4.6★ from 59K ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id778772892)). Free parcel boundaries and an easy interface ([Outdoor Life 2019](https://www.outdoorlife.com/tested-best-hunting-apps-for-hunters/) `[old]`).
- **Complaints:** Reviewers in Dec 2024 reported a **data breach notice**, hunt areas lost after an update, offline maps no longer working, and a clunkier interface ([unitQ scorecard](https://unitq.com/unitq-scorecards/huntstand)).

## Spartan Forge
- **What and who:** An AI deer-movement prediction app founded by a former Army intelligence officer. It is aimed at serious whitetail hunters ([Outdoor Life, 2021](https://www.outdoorlife.com/hunting/spartan-forge-hunting-app/) `[old]`).
- **Pricing:**
  - A free tier covers default maps, weather and pins. One paid membership covers all states, billed monthly or yearly ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1562873100)).
  - Third-party listings give conflicting prices: $7.99–$12.99/mo and $39.99–$79.99/yr ([Adapty paywall library](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/spartan-forge/)). Exact current price is `[unverified]`.
- **Key features:** Deer prediction trained on GPS-collar data, leaf-on and leaf-off imagery, LiDAR, crop layers, historical wind and weather, a journal, and the CyberScout AI assistant ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1562873100)).
- **Pin privacy:** In the Blue Force Tracker design, the person who draws a property polygon owns it and can restrict certain waypoints ([Outdoor Life](https://www.outdoorlife.com/hunting/spartan-forge-hunting-app/) `[old]`, described as "coming soon" in 2021). Current behavior is `[unverified]`.
- **Group use:** Pins can be shared by text. Blue Force Tracker handles team hunts ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1562873100)).
- **Harvest logging:** A journal logs conditions ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1562873100)). There is no dedicated harvest board `[unverified]`.
- **Weather and wind:** Hour-by-hour wind, a 10-day forecast and predictions that work offline ([Outdoor Life](https://www.outdoorlife.com/hunting/spartan-forge-hunting-app/) `[old]`).
- **Offline:** Offline maps and forecasts ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1562873100)).
- **Praise:** Reviewers like the prediction AI, historical weather, the journal, and an all-states price cheaper than onX ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1562873100)).
- **Complaints:** The free trial is unclear and requires a commitment. UAV imagery covers mostly the Midwest. Public-land layers trail onX ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1562873100)). The 65% accuracy claim comes from the founder's own study ([Outdoor Life](https://www.outdoorlife.com/hunting/spartan-forge-hunting-app/)).

## BaseMap
- **What and who:** A budget all-states hunting and fishing map with a social journal ([Hunting Life review](https://huntinglife.com/basemap-review) `[vendor: written by BaseMap staff]`).
- **Pricing:** Pro is $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr. Hunt Research is $9.99/yr. A Pro Bundle is $99.99/yr ([Adapty paywall library](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/basemap/)).
- **Key features:** 800+ layers, 3D maps (iOS only), a desktop planner, and **SmartMarkers that save local weather when a waypoint is saved** ([GearJunkie 2020](https://gearjunkie.com/outdoor/hunt-fish/basemap-hunting-app) `[vendor: sponsored] [old]`; [Hunting Life](https://huntinglife.com/basemap-review)).
- **Pin privacy:** Journal entries can be public, friends-only or private. Location sharing goes only to selected friends ([Hunting Life](https://huntinglife.com/basemap-review)).
- **Group use:** Real-time location sharing with chosen friends and waypoints sent by text or email. Both **need cell service** ([Hunting Life](https://huntinglife.com/basemap-review)).
- **Harvest logging:** The journal logs activities, tracks, photos and harvests ([GearJunkie](https://gearjunkie.com/outdoor/hunt-fish/basemap-hunting-app)).
- **Weather and wind:** Per-waypoint weather and sunrise/sunset times. Wind is not called out ([GearJunkie](https://gearjunkie.com/outdoor/hunt-fish/basemap-hunting-app)).
- **Offline:**
  - Unlimited offline map caching, best downloaded on Wi-Fi.
  - Larger areas download with less detail.
  - 3D does not work offline ([Hunting Life](https://huntinglife.com/basemap-review)).
- **Praise and complaints:** No independent review summary found `[unverified]`. Cons noted by the vendor's own review: sharing needs signal, and the planner is a paid add-on ([Hunting Life](https://huntinglife.com/basemap-review)).

## HuntWise
- **What and who:** A hunt-forecasting and mapping app from Sportsman Tracker. It is rated 4.7★ from about 69K ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id645518545)).
- **Pricing:** Pro is $19.99/mo or $59.99/yr. Elite is $39.99/mo or $119.99/yr ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id645518545)).
- **Key features:**
  - HuntCast gives a daily 1–10 "huntability" score from pressure, wind, moon phase and temperature. RutCast tracks rut phase by county.
  - WindCast scores saved stands against the day's wind, using the ideal wind directions the user sets ([HuntWise comparison](https://huntwise.com/field-guide/hunting-tips/huntwise-vs-huntstand-hunting-app-comparison); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id645518545)).
- **Pin privacy:** Private until shared. App Store privacy labels say purchases, contact info and usage data are used to track users across other companies' apps ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id645518545)).
- **Group use:** Save a hunt area, then tap **Invite**. Buddies see everyone's pins, markers and notes. Permissions are not documented ([HuntWise blog, Oct 2024](https://huntwise.com/field-guide/hunting-tips/the-hunting-app-buddy-system-share-nearby-hunting-areas-with-friends)).
- **Harvest logging:** A community "Log Feed." No harvest log is listed ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id645518545)).
- **Weather and wind:** Strongest of the five on per-stand wind (WindCast, Live Wind) ([HuntWise comparison](https://huntwise.com/field-guide/hunting-tips/huntwise-vs-huntstand-hunting-app-comparison)).
- **Offline:** The free tier gets one offline area. Pro gets unlimited ([HuntWise comparison](https://huntwise.com/field-guide/hunting-tips/huntwise-vs-huntstand-hunting-app-comparison)).
- **Praise:** 3D terrain, property lines for the price, markers and path recording ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id645518545)).
- **Complaints:**
  - The app **wouldn't open without internet**, offline downloads didn't finish, and recorded paths were erased when signal dropped.
  - A landowner raised concerns about exposing property boundaries to trespassers ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id645518545)).

## Recommendations
_Ranked by value to the crew relative to effort to build. These are proposals for the PM and Jason, not decisions._

| # | Type | Recommendation | Value | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do better | **Make the camp map private by design, not by a share action.** Competitors share copies by link, and onX offline shares stop syncing after edits. Deer Camp pins should belong to the camp, visible to members only, with no public-link option for pins at all. | High | Low (already in the brief and RLS plan) |
| 2 | Gap | **Harvest board linked to a pin, with a weather snapshot.** No app in the big five combines a harvest log, a stand pin and a weather record. BaseMap SmartMarkers come closest. Copy that auto-stamp idea: on save, record wind, temperature, pressure and moon phase from cache, and fill them in after sync if the phone was offline. | High | Medium |
| 3 | Do better | **The app must open and save with no signal.** HuntWise reviews show what happens when it doesn't (won't open offline, lost tracks). Test cold start in airplane mode as an acceptance test. | High | Medium |
| 4 | Copy | **HuntWise WindCast idea, simplified:** let each stand pin store "good winds," then show a green, yellow or red tag on the stand-assignment board for today's forecast. It's cheap to build and useful at camp. | Medium-High | Low-Medium |
| 5 | Do better | **Make stand sign-in as easy as a whiteboard.** Clubs go back to paper because members forget to sign in or out ([GON](https://forum.gon.com/threads/vsib-anyone-else-using-it.921401/page-2)). One-tap claim, auto-release at dark, and an "unclaimed" view that reads at a glance. | High | Medium |
| 6 | Avoid | **Don't compete on mapping layers, parcels or AI prediction.** That ground belongs to onX, HuntStand and Spartan Forge, and their users already pay for it. Let crew members keep using their scouting app, and offer "paste coordinates" or GPX import later `[proposal]`. | Saves effort | — |
| 7 | Avoid | **Updates that lose data, and weak account security** (HuntStand reviews). Use soft deletes, migrations with tests, and no stored passwords (the email-code default in D-2 helps). | High (trust) | Low |
| 8 | Proposal for Jason | Trail-cam photo import, scouting AI, and parcel/landowner lookup are **outside the brief**. Raise them only if the crew asks. | Low for the crew | High |
